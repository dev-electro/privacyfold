## Next-Generation JPEG Optimization

MozJPEG improves classical JPEG compression through trellis quantization, optimized Huffman tables, progressive scans, and smoothed luminance matrices.

PrivacyFold compiles MozJPEG to WebAssembly (`mozjpeg_enc.wasm`), bringing high-efficiency photographic compression directly to the browser.
