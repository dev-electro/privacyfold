## International Biometric Passport Standards

ICAO Doc 9303 defines universal parameters for biometric passports recognized by 193 United Nations member states.

Key requirements include:
- **Crown-to-Chin Ratio:** Facial height must occupy between 70% and 80% of the total frame height.
- **Eye Line Positioning:** Eye center line positioned between 50% and 70% from the bottom of the photo.
- **Background Uniformity:** Plain white or light grey background with zero shadows and neutral exposure.
