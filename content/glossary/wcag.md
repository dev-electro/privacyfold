## Digital Accessibility Standards

WCAG defines testable success criteria across three levels of conformance: A, AA, and AAA.

Key color contrast ratios:
- **WCAG Level AA:** Requires minimum 4.5:1 contrast for normal text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+).
- **WCAG Level AAA:** Requires 7:1 contrast for normal text and 4.5:1 for large text.
