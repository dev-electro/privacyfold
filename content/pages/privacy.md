# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 10, 2026

PrivacyFold operates under a fundamental technical guarantee: **we cannot see, store, or share your files because they never leave your device.**

---

## 1. File and Document Data
When you use any tool on PrivacyFold:
- Your files and text inputs are loaded directly into your browser's local RAM.
- Processing is performed by client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and WebCrypto.
- **Zero file bytes are transmitted over the internet.**
- When you close your browser tab, all memory buffers are instantly purged by the browser runtime.

---

## 2. Server Logs and Network Requests
PrivacyFold is hosted as a static site on Cloudflare Pages.
- Cloudflare may collect standard technical edge logs (IP addresses, request paths, user-agent headers) strictly for DDoS mitigation, security, and content delivery routing.
- PrivacyFold runs zero analytics scripts, zero tracking pixels, and zero user fingerprinting code.

---

## 3. Advertising and Cookies
To sustain our free, account-less tools, PrivacyFold displays advertisements via Google AdSense.
- By default, PrivacyFold requests **non-personalized ads** for all visitors.
- We do not share user identity, tool inputs, or document metadata with advertising networks.
- You can manage or modify your cookie and advertising preferences at any time via the Cookie Preferences link in the footer.
